## Do's and Don'ts
- Native buttons retain Space/Enter activation. Start and resume return focus to the court; pointer shot selection does the same. The court has a visible keyboard focus outline.
- A shot change updates any buffered stroke and recomputes the incoming contact height, including while paused. An unavailable low smash cancels the buffered stroke with guidance.
- Only cancellation or capture loss for the active pointer cancels its stroke. Pause, blur and reset release that capture; secondary pointer events cannot discard a primary swipe.
- Swept net collision includes both endpoints of the integration interval, including an exact arrival at or departure from the net plane.
- Keep the court, shuttle and timing window visible and central.
- Keep graphics grounded in badminton materials. Do not restore purple neon, colored beams, large-headed characters, thick black outlines, glossy title shadows or repeated motivational slogans.
- Keep the court singles boundary at CFG.halfWidth even when the full doubles mat is drawn; visual-only work must not change collision coordinates.
- Canvas ART consumes CSS tokens; do not introduce an independent venue palette.
- Validate against the actual physics trajectories and real pointer/touch input in Chromium.
- Distinguish measured reference behavior from new badminton-specific design decisions.
- Do not claim to use MATDRIVE's original engine or source assets.
- Do not introduce online progression before the local game is stable.
